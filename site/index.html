About me

My name is Mohamad Hairul Bin Ismail. I was born in Sarawak and currently live and study in Kota Kinabalu, Sabah. My passion for technology began during childhood, when I became fascinated by how things work, communicate, and connect with one another.

This curiosity inspired me to pursue Software Engineering, where I can deepen my knowledge and develop practical skills in an ever evolving industry. I am excited to explore new ideas, create meaningful solutions, and discover how I can contribute to the future of technology.

During my degree I discovered cloud computing, and I've been hooked since. Instead of only reading about it, I built my own portfolio on AWS the way real cloud teams work: infrastructure written as code, changes deployed by a pipeline, and code tested automatically.

During my free time, I used to play games a lot. Now that I am learning software engineering, I can enhance my passion for gaming not just by playing games, but also by creating one myself. Becoming a game developer is definitely one of my dream jobs, along with becoming a cloud engineer.
